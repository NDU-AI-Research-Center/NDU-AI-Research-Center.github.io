About Us

Solving Local Problems
with Intelligent Systems


The AI Research Centre of Ndejje University (NDU) is dedicated to delivering state-of-the-art AI-driven solutions to local challenges in Uganda and its communities. It is facilitated by the NDU Faculty of Science and Computing in cooperation with the German-based organisation Coworkers.

We are always open for exchange and cooperation — whether you are a researcher, student, institution, or industry partner.

Research Directions

Three Pillars of
Applied AI Research

⚡

Machine Learning in Energy Grid Stability

We apply machine learning approaches to optimise power distribution, reduce energy wastage, and ensure reliable electricity supply — contributing to economic development and improved living standards across Uganda.

🎓

AI in Education

We create innovative AI solutions that hold the potential to revolutionise Uganda's education system by providing personalised learning experiences, improving teacher–student ratios, and enhancing educational access and quality.

💬

Chatbot-based Service Delivery

We research chatbots that overcome geographical barriers, increase accessibility to essential services, and enhance the efficiency and convenience of public service interactions for Ugandan citizens.

AEPAS 2.0

AI-Powered Economic Alignment & Skills System

Audits university curricula against Uganda's National Development Plan (NDP III/IV) targets and NCHE/IUCEA benchmarks using Semantic AI. Acts as a "Market-Intelligence Layer" for faculties — identifying courses that are academically strong but economically outdated.

ALIGN
OPTIMISE
IMPACT REPORT

Generates an "Economic Readiness Certificate" — a Market-Alignment Score for every programme.

Strategic Impact for Uganda

Accelerated Market-Readiness
Ensures graduates possess action-oriented skills required to drive the economy.

Data-Driven Innovation
Identifies innovation gaps where the University can introduce high-demand modules.

Provides a market-alignment score for every programme, showing exactly how NDU contributes to national transformation.

UMEME Power Outage Duration Prediction

Predicts how long a reported power outage will last using a neural network trained on four weeks of scraped UMEME data. 95% test-set accuracy; 80% of predictions are within one hour of actual resolution time.

AI Advice Sessions

Level 1 · 30 min

Planning to use AI in a project and need guidance on which technology or algorithm to use? Want to discuss feasibility or just know how to get started? Book a 30-minute slot — describe your topic when requesting so we can assign a suitable advisor.

Level 2 · 60 min

Already conducting an AI-based project and facing a technical problem? Book a 60-minute deep-dive with one of our experts. Describe your topic so we can match you to the best person for your challenge.
